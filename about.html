About us

We build the face of your startup before you’ve made it.

Horizon Symmetry is a digital presence studio in Madrid. We work with early-stage founders who need to look established before the first pitch, the first press mention, the first enterprise contract.

One team covers the whole surface: identity, website, product, and the systems behind them. Nothing is lost in a handoff between a branding agency, a developer, and a marketing freelancer who have never spoken.

Your digital presence is not a deliverable. It is a position. We help you occupy it early.

What we do

  1. 01

    Web design and development

    Interactive, performant sites that make a first impression hold. Built for founders, optimised for trust rather than for a template gallery.

  2. 02

    Brand identity and visual systems

    Logo, type, colour and motion, coherent from day one. The visual language that says what the words alone cannot.

  3. 03

    Digital strategy and positioning

    Messaging architecture, founder narrative, investor-ready framing. Your story, told with precision.

How we work

  1. Discover

    Map the vision, the market context, and what your audience already expects, before a single pixel is placed.

  2. Design

    Brand direction, visual language, and layout architecture aligned to your stage rather than to someone else’s.

  3. Build

    Development with precision: performant, responsive, and refined down to the last interaction.

  4. Launch

    Deployed fast, handed over documented, and ready to scale without us in the room.
