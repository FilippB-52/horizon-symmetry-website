KOMNATA

A room you are let into

2025

KOMNATA started as a Russian party concept and was opening to a wider Madrid crowd. The audience arrives through Instagram, at night, on a phone, at speed.

That decided the brief. Recognition had to happen in about a second, in a feed, at the size of a thumb.

A room you are let into, not a club you queue for. The name means room and the brand runs on it: closed door, known crowd, something already happening inside.

Held deliberately away from the polished nightlife look: intimate, not VIP.

Deep blue against black, high-contrast poster typography, and a lineup format that stays legible after three reposts.

Built for volume: every night needs a poster, and posters that take a week do not get made.

We ran the social output and designed the language of the events: poster series, DJ lineups, teasers, and the Broadcast Night identity.

Templates and a small kit of parts, so a night's assets ship in an afternoon.
