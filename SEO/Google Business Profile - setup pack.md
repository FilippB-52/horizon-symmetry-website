# Google Business Profile, setup pack

Everything below is ready to paste. Created 2026-09-17.
This is the single biggest free lever we have: Google Business Profile drives
roughly a third of all local pack ranking signals, and the local pack sits
above the organic results for every "diseño web Madrid" style query.

Start at https://business.google.com

---

## THE WALKTHROUGH, step by step

You are not applying for SEO. There is nothing to register for and nothing to
pay. This is claiming a free Google listing, the map pin with the name, hours
and reviews that shows when you search a restaurant. No approval beyond Google
checking the business is real.

**1.** Go to https://www.google.com/business/ and sign in with
   horizonsymmetrystudio@gmail.com, NOT a personal account. Whoever is signed
   in owns the listing.

**2.** "Add your business". Name exactly: Horizon Symmetry. Nothing appended.
   Adding "Diseño Web Madrid" to the name gets listings suspended.

**3.** Business type: Diseñador de sitios web. Pick it from the dropdown.

**4.** "Add a location customers can visit?" Answer NO. This is what makes it
   a service area business and keeps the address private.

**5.** Where do you serve: Madrid, then add Comunidad de Madrid.

**6.** Website horizonsymmetry.com, plus a real phone number that gets answered.

**7.** Verification. It asks for the home address here, never published. Google
   usually wants a short video: the workspace, the street or building entrance,
   and proof the business is yours (laptop with the site open, or a document
   with the Zencup name). Have it ready before starting, this is the step that
   catches people out.

**8.** Submit and wait, a few days to a few weeks.

Once verified: paste the description from section 4 above, add the four
services from section 5, upload the photos from section 7, set real hours.
Then ask Ar/Cut, CRUDE and KOMNATA for reviews.

## 1. Business name

    Horizon Symmetry

Exactly that, nothing appended. Do NOT write "Horizon Symmetry | Diseño Web

violation and one of the most common causes of profile suspension.

---

## 2. Categories

Primary category, this is the heaviest single setting on the whole profile:

    Diseñador de sitios web

Additional categories, add all four:

    Diseñador gráfico
    Agencia de marketing
    Empresa de software
    Servicio de diseño de logotipos

Note the tension here and decide knowingly: the agency is four pillars at
equal weight, but the primary category can only be one thing, and web design
is the query you said you wanted to win. The additional categories carry the
other three pillars. If the priority ever changes, the primary is editable.

---

## 3. Address and service area

Choose "I deliver goods and services to my customers" and then tick
"Hide my address, I serve customers at their location."

Verify with the Madrid address you actually work from. It is never shown
publicly on a service area profile, it is only used to prove the business is
real and to anchor it to the city.

Do not use Calle de Lagasca 63, 4A. It is the company's registered address,
and the studio has worked remotely since July 2026, so there is no office to
verify. Google detects registered agent and virtual office addresses by
address clustering and suspends the profiles that use them. Verify with the
home address instead; a service area profile never shows it.

Service area, add both:

    Madrid
    Comunidad de Madrid

## 7. Photos

Profiles with photos get materially more engagement, and engagement is a
ranking signal. Upload at minimum:

- Logo: assets/favicon-512.png
- Cover: assets/hero-reveal-1440.jpg
- Work: assets/work/arcut.jpg, assets/work/crude.jpg, assets/work/komnata.jpg
- Team: assets/about-1.jpg

Rename each file to something descriptive before uploading, for example
`diseno-web-madrid-horizon-symmetry.jpg`. The filename is read.
